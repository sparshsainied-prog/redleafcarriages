Redleaf Farm, Fitzroy Falls, Southern Highlands

Redleaf Carriages. Sleep in an old railway carriage on a working farm.

Carriage 1238 is a 1920s passenger car. Carriage 343 is a guards van built in 1890. Both sit in the paddocks, just under two hours from Sydney.

Check datesSee both carriages

The king room

In 1238 the king bed has a compartment to itself, with a chandelier and the original sliding doors.

Two carriages, two paddocks

Katrina and Sam run Redleaf with their four kids. They grow free range pigs, chickens, sheep and beef cattle, and each carriage is fully self contained, with all the linen provided.

1238The family one

A 1920s passenger car close to the house. King room, bunk room, sitting room, kitchenette and a deck over the paddocks. Sleeps 5.

343The secluded one

An 1890 guards van on its original wheels, alone in a 20 acre paddock. Solar powered, with a zinc bath under the scribbly gums. Sleeps 4.

Carriage 1238A 1920s passenger car

It keeps its old timber inside, complete with almost 100 years of graffiti. It's built on a side-aisle plan: three compartments at one end, an open living space and kitchenette at the other, and the original toilet compartments at either end, one now a shower.

Sleeps
Up to 5. King room, and a bunk room with up to three single bunks ("kids love it").
Living
Sitting room, dining table and a kitchenette with an oven, stove and mini fridge.
Outside
A deck over the paddocks, with highland cows, sheep and chickens to watch, and a barbecue.
Wake-up
The roosters.
Good to know
You drive through a paddock and a few gates to get there. No TV; wifi is free but a digital detox is encouraged. Pack warm clothes, it can be cold any time of year.

Carriage 343An 1890 guards van

Modified in 1930 with a freight room, guards van and passenger seating, and still on its original wheels. It sits in its own 20 acre paddock, rural views on one side, bush and scribbly gums on the other.

Sleeps
Up to 4. The old freight van is the queen bedroom, with antiqued mirror walls and Juliet balconies through the parcel doors, plus two single bunks.
The bath
A French zinc bath outside, with a sunset bush view under the gums.
Warmth
The original guard's fuel stove makes a small fireplace. Sheepskin rugs and warm doonas.
Kitchen
A 1910 Early Kooka gas stove and oven, a small solar fridge, and a fire pit with a barbecue plate outside.
Off grid
Solar power and creek water, with drinking water supplied. No TV, hairdryer or microwave.
On arrival
Ask Katrina to arrange a grazing box and you won't have to leave.

A working farm

From 1238's deck you can watch highland cows, sheep and chickens. Katrina and Sam raise their animals ethically and sustainably, and sell the meat as Redleaf Pork, Lamb and Beef.

  • Highland cows, sheep, goats, chickens and the odd peacock.
  • State forest next door, with mountain bike and walking tracks at the doorstep.
  • Just under two hours from Sydney.
  • It's a working farm: please close the gates behind you.
